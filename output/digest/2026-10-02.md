3. *Comp reality: the ₹15L line is a role split, not a seniority ladder*
   2026 India bands: BI Developer ₹8–15L (plateauing) vs Analytics Engineer ₹12–22L and Data Engineer ₹14–28L; SQL+Python+cloud ₹8–30L vs Power BI+SQL ₹6–20L. A services firm pays a 3–5 yr analyst ₹7–12L where a product firm pays ₹15–28L for a comparable profile.
   Why it matters: same title, ~2× spread — cross ₹15L by moving to the product/GCC side and the Python+cloud stack, not by adding years.
   https://resumevera.com/in/salary-guide/data-analyst · https://dattasable.com/blog/microsoft-fabric-career-roadmap-2026

*Also worth a glance:* Fabric certs to hold are now DP-600 (Analytics Engineer), DP-700 (Data Engineer), DP-800 (SQL AI Developer); the same roadmap tags SQL AI Developer the "exponential" role at ₹16–32L (vendor blog — directional, not gospel).
