## Sourcing notes

- **qanomed is down this run.** It served Agra and Varanasi both a `http=522` (Cloudflare edge
  timeout), zero bytes, on two attempts. It was the best phone-bearing source of 2026-10-01, so the
  loss is worth noting, but it is a source failure not a city failure.
- **AskLaila worked and was city correct, unlike Vadodara.** The Agra page
  (`/search/Agra/-/dentist/`) returned 19 listings with landlines all starting `0562`, and the
  Varanasi page (`/search/Varanasi/-/dentist/`) returned 16 listings with landlines all starting
  `0542`. Both match the cities' STD codes, so the wrong-city trap that poisoned Vadodara on
  2026-09-30 and 2026-10-01 does not apply here. The STD-code guard held and is still the cheap check
  to run first.
- **Method, same as last run.** Every candidate got a DNS sweep of name-derived domains (name plus
  .com/.in/.co.in/.net/.org/.clinic) and then a name search. A lead was filed only when nothing under
  the clinic name resolved to a live page owned by that clinic and the name search returned
  directories alone. The four domains that did resolve were fetched and matched against the wrong city,
  and dropped: srikrishnadentalclinic.com (Hyderabad), dentalspecialityclinic.com (Des Moines WA),
  dentalspeciality.com (no page served), pratapdental.in (Gorakhpur), pratapdentalclinic.com (Deoghar),
  chaddhaclinic.com (no page served, and chaddha.com is a surname site).
- **Closed this run:** qanomed (522). Justdial, Sulekha and the other 403 directories from prior runs
  were not re-tested, since WebSearch plus AskLaila plus the DNS sweep already covered the pool.
