## Sourcing notes

- **Bhubaneswar returned zero leads, and the reason is access, not the city.** Every phone bearing
  source for it was closed this run. Justdial and bharatibiz both returned 403 (and bharatibiz also
  403 to the Googlebot user agent and to a reader proxy, which met a CAPTCHA wall), Sulekha served a
  page but hides every phone behind a login, Lybrate serves clinic pages but masks the clinic number
  and shows only its own customer service line (90155 59900), cybo returned 403, and AskLaila served
  503 for both the search and listing URLs. OpenStreetMap returned 128 dentist POIs across both
  cities but not one carried a phone tag. Sulekha was good enough to list Bhubaneswar clinic names
  (Utkal Dental Care, Omm Dental Clinic, Kedia Dental, Sai Lazer Dental, Brite Smile, Confident Smile,
  Lingaraj Laser, Oracare, Dental Implants Clinic Patia) but with no contact path, so per the rule
  "no contact path, no lead" none were filed.
- **What worked.** assamhealthcare.coop, an Assam healthcare cooperative member directory, served
  clean HTML with clinic name, address, phone and representative for its two dental members, and it
  is Guwahati correct. top-rated.online mirrors Google Places data and served one Guwahati clinic
  with phone and no website field. Both are tier 2 3 friendly.
- **What is still closed.** Justdial, Sulekha, bharatibiz, cybo, AskLaila (503 this run, previously
  served Agra, Varanasi, Ludhiana and Surat). qanomed, the best source of 2026-10-01, is still down,
  second run running, http 522. Search engines (DuckDuckGo, Mojeek) remain closed to automated reads,
  so all discovery came from the WebSearch tool inside its 4 call cap.
- **Method for the no-site signal, unchanged.** Every candidate got a DNS sweep of name derived
  domains (name plus .com/.in/.co.in/.net/.org/.clinic/.care), and any that resolved was fetched and
  checked for the clinic's city and phone. A lead was filed only when nothing under the clinic name
  resolved to a live page owned by that clinic and a name search returned directories alone. The
  known weakness holds, a clinic on an unrelated domain would be a false positive, so the 60 second
  phone check before sending still applies.
- **Signal tested against, and killed, other Guwahati and Bhubaneswar clinics.** Smile Xone Dental
  Hospital has its own site (smilexone.in, Bhubaneswar and Bhanjanagar) so it is not a lead. Sai Ansh
  Dental Clinic (Bhubaneswar) has no owned domain but its phone is masked on Lybrate, so no contact
  path, not filed. Matrushree Dental Clinic (Chandrasekharpur, Dr Pragyan Prasad) same problem, the
  only number on its page was Lybrate's own. Dent Inn Family Dental Care (Bhubaneswar) is ambiguous,
  dentinn.in resolves but 403 to every user agent tried, so it may be their site behind a firewall,
  and a 403 is not proof of a broken site, not filed. Elite Dental Clinic (Bhubaneswar) was left out
  because the name is generic and every same name domain found belongs to a Pune, US or unnamed
  practice, so a no-site call could not be made safely. maxz and smile xone pages on dentistinsider.in
  both showed the same number 9671405067, which is the directory's own, not a clinic's, so they were
  not used.
