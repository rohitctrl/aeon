## How hot is measured

- **P1** = no website of its own at all + a working contact path (hottest, cleanest pitch).
- **P2** = site broken/abandoned/parked.
- Ranked within P1 by service value (implant / oral surgery / hospital-scale) and by proof
  of a real patient base (Google reviews, registry presence). WhatsApp-viable mobiles first.

Result: **20 leads, 19 P1 + 1 P2.** Cities: Guwahati, Varanasi, Patna, Agra, Ludhiana,
Surat, Lucknow, Nashik, Bhopal.
