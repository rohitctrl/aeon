## Sourcing notes

- **Live source this run was healthfrog.in, reachable but slow.** Its `/doctor/` pages do not answer within a
  normal timeout over bash curl; they returned `http=000` (timeout) at 20s to 45s, then answered cleanly at
  10s to 64s once the timeout was raised to 60/90s. So the whole run is curl based, one polite fetch per page,
  no scraping loop. The root page and both city pages answered; eight clinic detail pages answered with no
  CAPTCHA. The wall that throttled the 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06 runs did **not** trip this run, so healthfrog
  is working again **and no broken-source notification is due**.
- **Two city index pages were read (Ranchi 50 listings, Madurai 48), and eight clinic detail pages were
  fetched** (four per city). Five were filed; three were reached, cleared the checks, and were held back only
  to respect the five-lead cap, listed here so a future run does not re-fetch them blind: Deb's Oral & Dental
  Care (102 Commerce Tower, Bel Air Lane, Ranchi, 9431103500 mobile, 0651 2330087 landline), Paramkamal Dental
  Clinic & Implant Center (Hinoo Birsa Chowk, Ranchi, 9472770498 / 8271852428), and A. A. Sastha Dental &
  Orthodontic Centre (No.10 Chinna Chokkikulam, Madurai, 0452 2536576 landline only). All three carry a clean
  no-site signal and could be filed on a later run.
- **The DNS sweep is the no-site signal, and its known weakness still holds.** Some clinics share a generic
  name whose same-name domain belongs to someone else. Those were dropped this run rather than risk a wrong
  call: Dental Solutions (dentalsolutions.in/.co.in/.net/.org all resolve, none is the Ranchi clinic), Dental
  Concepts (four TLDs resolve), Smiles Central (two resolve), and Sun Dental Care (three resolve). Dropping them
  is the conservative choice; each would need the 60 second phone check before a send anyway.
- **Ranchi and Madurai were never scouted before this run**, so no prior-run comparison exists. The batch is
  drawn from page one of each city listing only; both directories are paginated and the rest was not read
  inside budget.
- **Source data:** healthfrog.in Ranchi and Madurai dentist directories (one city listing page each, eight
  clinic detail pages), a DNS sweep of name-derived domains for 46 candidate clinics, HTTP reachability on the
  source, and three name-check WebSearch calls. No contact made anywhere, one page per directory, nothing
  written outside this repo.
- **Housekeeping:** this run's scratch files (`.parse_hf.py`, `.extract_listings.py`, `.dns_sweep.py`,
  `.ranchi.json`, `.madurai.json`) are dot prefixed and gitignored under `output/dental-leads/.*`, safe to
  remove by hand.
