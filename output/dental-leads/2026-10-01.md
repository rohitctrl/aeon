## Sourcing notes

- **AskLaila's Vadodara page is serving Bangalore data, again.** Same trap as 2026-09-30, now
  confirmed twice. `https://www.asklaila.com/search/Vadodara/-/dentist/` returned 20 listings whose
  landlines all begin `080` (Bangalore) and whose page carries the text "Bangalore Locality Guide".
  Vadodara's STD code is `0265`. So AskLaila cannot source Vadodara at all, and the city URL is
  actively misleading. The guard from last run holds and is cheap: check the landline STD code
  against the city before filing anything. Ludhiana's page is sound in that respect (only `0161`
  numbers, Ludhiana localities) but thin, it lists 2 businesses.
- **Search engines closed to automated reads this run.** DuckDuckGo's html and lite endpoints both
  returned a 202 anti-bot page with zero results, Mojeek returned 403, and Bing was not retried
  after last run's base64 wrapping. So all verification this run had to come from the WebSearch
  tool (4 used, at cap) plus direct fetching of individual clinic pages.
- **What worked as a source.** qanomed (`https://qanomed.com/clinics/list/India/Ludhiana`) embeds a
  clean clinic JSON blob in its HTML, 22 Ludhiana businesses with name and phone, 11 of them
  dental. That was the single best phone-bearing source of the run, better than AskLaila for this
  city. healthfrog inpages and meraludhiana also served fully.
- **What is still closed.** Justdial, Sulekha, indiabiz (403 even to a Googlebot UA), bharatibiz
  (403), mapcarta (403), Grotal (404 last run). AskLaila open for Surat/Ludhiana, poisoned for
  Vadodara.
- **Method for the no-site signal, and its weakness.** Every candidate was run through a DNS sweep
  of name-derived domains (name plus .com/.in/.co.in/.net/.org/.clinic), and any that resolved was
  fetched and checked for the clinic's own city as well as for the clinic's phone number appearing
  on the page. A lead was only filed when nothing under the clinic name resolved to a live page.
  The weakness is real and worth naming: absence of a guessing-derived domain is not proof of
  absence of a site, a clinic can use an unrelated domain. It is good enough when combined with a
  name search returning only directory pages, which is what happened for leads 1 and 2, and it is
  why leads 1 and 2 are the confident ones.
- **The signal was tested against, and killed, six other clinics.** These all had a live page under
  their own name and were dropped, not filed: Dental Roots (dentalrootsludhiana.com),
  The Tooth Place (thetoothplace.co.in, the filed phone 9815366274 appears on the page so the match
  is confirmed), Dr Vivek Saggar (viveksaggar.com, a Ludhiana implant site),
  Thind/Danish Thind (thinddentalclinic.com, and its number 92568 92568 matches the Danish Thind
  listing exactly), Sharma Dental Clinic (sharmadentalclinicludhiana.com), Mittal HiTech
  (mittaldentalcare.com).
