## Sourcing notes

This run took most of its time re-finding a working source, and two of the findings are
worth keeping.

- **AskLaila's Vadodara page is serving Bangalore data.** The Surat page
  (`/search/Surat/-/dentist/`) is sound: every landline in it starts `0261`, which is
  Surat's area code, and the localities are Surat localities. The Vadodara page
  (`/search/Vadodara/-/dentist/`) returned 20 listings whose localities are *all*
  Bangalore (Basavanagudi, BTM 2nd Stage, HSR Layout, Bellandur, Electronic City,
  Koramangala, Indira Nagar, Ulsoor, Yelahanka, J P Nagar, Madiwala) and whose landlines
  are all `080`, Bangalore's code. So AskLaila cannot be used to source Vadodara at all,
  and the same trap will catch any future run that trusts a city URL over the area code.
  The cheap guard is to check the landline's STD code against the city before filing.
- **OpenStreetMap is not usable for these two cities.** The Overpass extract for Surat
  and Vadodara returned 89 dentists, and **zero of them carry a `phone` tag and zero
  carry a `website` tag** (the tags present are name, address, `source`; 84 of 89 are a
  single "Local Knowledge" import). The no-website signal that worked for Lucknow and
  Patna last time is therefore an artifact of that import here, not evidence about any
  individual clinic, and filing OSM leads from these cities would have been wrong. No OSM
  lead was filed.
- **Directories that are open vs closed this run.** Open and parseable: AskLaila,
  healthfrog. Closed to automated reads: Justdial, Sulekha, Cybo, Grotal (404 page),
  polomap, clickedindia, bharatibiz, yellowpages.in, r.jina.ai. Bing returns
  base64-wrapped result URLs and irrelevant results for these queries. DuckDuckGo's HTML
  endpoint works but rate-limits after roughly a dozen queries, which is what capped
  verification at two clinics.
- **Method:** leads were sourced from AskLaila Surat (real phone numbers read straight
  out of the listing HTML), then the no-website signal was checked by searching the exact
  clinic name and looking for a name-derived domain among the results. Both filed leads
  returned only third party directories, no own domain.
