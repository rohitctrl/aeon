## Sourcing notes

- **healthfrog.in was the source, and it rate limited mid run again.** Its Andhra Pradesh dentist pages
  carry clean HTML with clinic name, address, landline and mobile, and its city listing pages
  (`/doctor/dentists/andhra-pradesh/vijayawada` and `/andhra-pradesh/visakhapatnam`) each returned 35 and
  38 dentist names. Six Vijayawada clinic pages and four Visakhapatnam clinic pages were read before the
  wall: from the tenth detail fetch onward the URL 307 redirected to a "Human Verification" page (http
  200 with a CAPTCHA body). All four Vijayawada leads and the one Visakhapatnam lead were captured before
  the block. This is a **single source miss, not two in a row**, and leads were filed, so per the skill no
  broken source notification was sent.
- **Visakhapatnam returned only one lead, and the reason is access, not the city.** Only three
  Visakhapatnam clinic pages (Caredental, Cure Dental, O-zone) were read before the CAPTCHA, and two of
  those three were killed on the name check (below). The remaining thirty five Visakhapatnam names were
  read on the listing but their phones sit on detail pages that were blocked. That name list is at the
  end of this note for a future run once healthfrog is reachable again or a second source is found.
