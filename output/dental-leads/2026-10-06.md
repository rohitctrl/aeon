  (`/doctor/dentists/maharashtra/pune`) returned 50 doctor links, 34 of them dental, in one fetch, and its
  clinic detail pages carry clean HTML with name, address, mobile (`fa-mobile`) and landline (`fa-phone`).
  Eight Pune clinic pages were read before the wall: from the ninth detail fetch on the URL redirected to a
  "Human Verification" page (http 200 with a CAPTCHA body), and the three remaining planned fetches all came
  back as that page. The wall now trips at roughly eight detail pages per run, down from roughly ten on
  2026-10-05 and twenty on 2026-10-04, so healthfrog is being throttled harder over time. This is a **single
  source miss, not two in a row**, and leads were filed, so per the skill no broken source notification was
