## Operator notes

- **Verify the no-website signal before sending.** All four are "no website
  recorded in OpenStreetMap", not "confirmed no website". OSM is crowd sourced
  and a missing `website` tag is weak evidence. A 60 second Google on the clinic
  name is enough. The drafts are phrased "I could not find a website", which
  stays true either way, but confirm before you send so you are not caught out.
- **Phone freshness.** These numbers come from a public map, not from the clinic.
  Expect some churn, especially on the two single-doctor entries.
